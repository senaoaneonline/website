📘 How-to Guides

Write a CV that works

  • Keep it to 1–2 pages with clear headings.
  • Add volunteer, church, school or stokvel work if you have no experience.
  • Save it as Name_CV.pdf and edit it for every job.
  • Do not put your ID number on a public CV.

Apply for Soweto jobs

Search daily for “General Worker Soweto 1804” and “Staff Soweto”. Walk in with a CV and certified ID copy during the employer’s advertised hours. Never pay to apply for a job.

Check SASSA status

Use the official SRD website or SASSA channels only. Do not share your PIN or pay an agent. Call SASSA on 0800 60 10 11 for official assistance.

Official SRD status →

Water outage preparation

Store safe water before an outage, fill buckets while supply is available, and follow official Joburg Water updates. Call 011 688 1400 to report problems or ask for verified tanker information.

Soweto staff vacancies

Check official careers pages for banks, retailers, malls, schools and clinics. Prepare a PDF CV, certified ID copy and proof of address. Confirm every vacancy directly with the employer.

Taxi safety: Senaoane to Bara

Ask the fare before boarding, keep valuables secure, use recognised ranks and avoid travelling alone late at night. Fares change, so confirm current prices locally.

❓ Community Q&A

Can I get a job without matric?

Some general worker, cleaning, retail, security and driver roles accept applicants without matric. Always check the individual advert and requirements.

Where can I report a water problem?

Contact Joburg Water on 011 688 1400 and use official City of Johannesburg updates for verified tanker information.

Are online courses really free?

Some courses are free, while certificates or advanced features may cost money. Read the provider’s current terms before enrolling.
