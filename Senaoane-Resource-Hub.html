📘 HOW-TO GUIDES - Everyday Problems

1. How to Write a CV that Works in SA (2026)
  • Keep 1-2 pages, use clear headings: Personal, Objective, Education, Experience, Skills, References
  • For no experience: add church, school, volunteer, stokvel work
  • Spell check! Edit for each job. Save as PDF: Name_CV.pdf
  • Include: ID? No! Only phone, address (Senaoane), email
Use Free Canva CV →
2. How to Apply for General Worker Jobs (Maponya/Jabulani)

Walk-in with CV at HR desk 09:00-11:00 Mon-Thu. Ask for "General Worker application". Take certified ID copy. Check Indeed "Soweto 1804" daily. WhatsApp CV to Senaoane News +27 65 332 7762 to share.

Source: Indeed, Jooble 2,997 vacancies Sept 2026

3. How to Check SASSA Status Without Queuing

Use SASSA WhatsApp: 082 046 8553 → type "Status". Or SRD site: srd.sassa.gov.za. Payment dates Sept 2026: Old Age 2nd, Disability 3rd, Children 4th. Don't rush day 1 - money stays 3 months.

4. How to Survive Water Outage (Phiri/Senaoane)

Buy 2x5L bottles now, refill every 2 months. Fill buckets when water flows. Cook: braai, pasta salad. Bowsers only after 24hrs. Follow @JHBWater, call 011 688 1400. Store 5L per person.

Source: IOL

5. How to Fix Gate Battery During Load Reduction

Charge full when power ON. Test monthly: switch off power, gate must still open 10x. If not, replace battery R350 at Maponya. Keep manual key safe. Keep small cash - ATMs die.

6. How to Use Bara Taxi Rank Safely

Senaoane → Bara ~10.99km, 17min. Finger up = CBD, down = Soweto. Don't bang door, no food inside. First taxi 05:00, last 21:00. Jabulani Mall rank every 10min.

❓ Q&A - Community Questions

Q: I have no matric - can I still get job in Soweto?

Yes! 52+ jobs with no matric now (Glassdoor Sept 2026). Focus on General Worker, Cleaner, Cashier, Security (PSIRA), Driver with PDP. Mr Price, Pikitup, Tsebo hire no matric. Add skills: computer, customer service.

Q: Where do I get water when Joburg Water cuts?

Tankers deployed at Phiri Hall, Senaoane Circle after 24hrs. Store 5L per person before cut. Call 011 688 1400. Follow WhatsApp group for tank location.

Q: How much is taxi Senaoane to Bara?

Minibus R20-R25 (2026). Meter taxi ~R110 for 10.99km. Always ask price before boarding. Morning rush 06:00-08:00 very full.

Q: Are free courses really free? Will I get certificate?

Yes - Google Digital Garage free with certificate. Coursera audit free (no certificate) or apply financial aid to get certificate free (UCT, Google IT). Alison free. Watch out - some sites charge - use links below.
