CV HELP

How to write a CV that gets noticed

1. Put your strongest information at the top: name, phone, email, location, and a short profile.

2. Keep it one page if possible, with clear headings: personal details, experience, education, skills, references.

3. Match your CV to the job, especially for retail, admin, cleaning, security, call centre, and warehouse work.

4. Add a short motivation sentence explaining why you want the job and why you fit it.

5. Save the file as a PDF and send it with a short message on WhatsApp or email.

JOB GUIDES

How to find work and apply without wasting time

Retail & sales

Apply with a neat CV, show customer service experience, and mention if you can work weekends.

General worker

Highlight physically active work, reliability, teamwork, and ability to follow instructions.

Security / cleaner

Be ready with ID, references, and proof of availability for early shifts or night work.

Office admin

Show your communication, Excel, filing, and phone skills. Basic computer literacy matters.

SASSA HELP

SASSA status check: what to do quickly

Check status: Use the official SASSA website or visit your nearest SASSA office. Keep your ID number ready.

If your grant is delayed: Confirm that your banking details, phone number and address are correct.

Before visiting: Save the reference number, call the SASSA helpline if needed, and ask exactly what is pending.

Common issues: wrong banking info, identity verification, pending review, or an update not yet processed.

WATER OUTAGES
  • Store enough water in clean containers.
  • Keep a bucket and small container for flushing and washing.
  • Use water sparingly during outage periods.
  • Check local community WhatsApp groups for updates.
TAXI / TRAVEL

Ask local drivers about the best route to work, school, clinic and shopping areas. Save cash, keep your ID and fare money safe, and share travel updates with your community group.

COMMUNITY Q&A

Common questions from the community

Where can I get help writing a CV?

Use a simple format, list your relevant work and skills, then keep the CV short and neat. You can also ask a local community group for a template.

What if I do not have matric?

Many jobs focus on practical skills, work ethic, and reliability. You can still apply for general worker, retail, cleaning, security, and support roles.

How do I check my SASSA payment status?

Use the official SASSA portal, check your banking details, and confirm your latest grant updates with the correct ID and cellphone number.

Where can I find free online courses?

Look at Google Digital Skills Africa, Alison, Coursera, and other free certificate programs that offer practical skills in office work and digital literacy.
