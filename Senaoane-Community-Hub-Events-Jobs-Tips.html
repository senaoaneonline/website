🚰 WATER OUTAGE SURVIVAL - SOWETO TIPS

  • ✅ Store early: Buy 2x 5L bottles, use & refill every 2 months - don't wait for Joburg Water SMS
  • ✅ Buckets: Fill during supply - keep for toilet flushing
  • ✅ Cook clever: Braai, pasta salads, bunny chow - no endless rinsing
  • ✅ Tankers: Joburg Water bowsers only after 24hrs - ration first day
  • ✅ Check: Follow @JHBWater on X + 011 688 1400

Source: IOL - How to survive JHB water outage

💡 LOAD REDUCTION / LOADSHEDDING TIPS

  • 💵 Keep small cash - ATMs die
  • 🔦 Backup lights + candles + rechargeable bulb (R80 at Maponya)

🚌 TAXI GUIDE - SENAOANE TO TOWN

Senaoane → Bara City Taxi Rank: ~10.99km, ~17min, ~R110 by meter taxi. Minibus taxi cheaper - ask at Senaoane Circle rank.

Jabulani Mall → Bara: Regular minibus every 10min from 05:00-21:00

Hand signals: Finger up = Joburg CBD, finger down = Soweto / Bara

Safety: Don't bang door, no eating in taxi, sit front if you have bags.
