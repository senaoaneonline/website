History Beckons: Orlando Pirates Chase Fifth Straight MTN8 Crown Against Sundowns on Saturday

Source: The South African / Time Out Durban • Final: Saturday 10 October, 18:00

Soweto's Orlando Pirates are two days away from a date with history. The Buccaneers face Mamelodi Sundowns in the 2026 MTN8 final at Moses Mabhida Stadium in Durban on Saturday, 10 October (kick-off 18:00) - chasing an unprecedented fifth successive MTN8 title.

Saturday's showdown is Pirates' 21st Top Eight final - drawing them level with Kaizer Chiefs for the most final appearances in the competition's history. A win would also see the Buccaneers draw level with Amakhosi on 15 titles at the top of the all-time winners' list.

Coach Abdeslam Ouaddou and vice-captain Tapelo Xoki addressed the media this week, with the squad in high spirits after a strong start to the season. Sundowns, under Miguel Cardoso, are equally determined to deny the Sea Robbers their historic five-peat.

eThekwini Mayor Cyril Xaba confirmed the city is ready: "We are working closely with the PSL, stadium management, law enforcement and emergency services to ensure arrangements are in place for match day." Tickets are R150 via Open Tickets, limited to 4 per person - every spectator, including children, needs a valid ticket.

The final kicks off a monster month for Pirates: CAF Champions League ties against African Stars of Namibia on 17 and 24 October, before the Soweto Derby against Kaizer Chiefs at FNB Stadium on 31 October (15:30).

Travel tip: Supporters heading to Durban are urged to plan journeys in advance, use public transport where possible, and follow instructions from officials. Once Always! ☠️
