Pitso Returns: Bafana Bafana Turn New Leaf Against Guinea - Wary of Guirassy Threat

Source: Sowetan / IOL • Pitso Mosimane Era Begins

As Bafana Bafana starts a new era under Pitso Mosimane with an Africa Cup of Nations qualifier against Guinea at Orlando Stadium on Saturday (3pm), the coach is well aware of the threat posed by Serhou Guirassy.

Mosimane conceded he and his team had spent sleepless nights devising a strategy to stop Guirassy, who plays for Borussia Dortmund and was leading scorer in the Champions League two seasons ago with 13 goals.

"They have a top striker, playing in Dortmund and scoring. We have to make sure we deal with his aerial strength," Mosimane said. "You know the West African team sheets. But the Cup of Nations has told you that it is not about where you play; it is about what happens on the pitch."

With SA's best defender, Mbekezeli Mbokazi, out with injury, Mosimane is expected to deploy Ime Okon or Olwethu Makhanya. Guinea arrive out of form - winless in last four.

Mosimane, 62, returns with a lot to prove after his first spell (2010-2012) failed to qualify for AFCON. Now a multiple CAF Champions League winner, he says: "We have to do better than we did in the last Afcon, to move better. It's too long without a cup. I want to win a cup."

He clarified: "We didn't sack anybody" - his new technical team was assembled after Hugo Broos' entire staff departed. He will rely on Mamelodi Sundowns core - Ronwen Williams, Teboho Mokoena, Themba Zwane.

Kick-off: Today 27 Sept, 15:00 at Orlando Stadium, Soweto. Bafana in Group for AFCON 2025 Morocco.
