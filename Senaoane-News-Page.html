Woman Found Dead in Thokoza Park Dam, Soweto - Police Open Inquest Docket

Source: IOL News / SABC News / SAPS Gauteng

Gauteng police have opened an inquest docket after the body of an unidentified woman was found wrapped in a duvet in a dam at Moroka, Soweto.

Police said the body was discovered on Sunday, September 20, 2026 at about 5pm in Thokoza Park, which includes Moroka Dam in Rockville. The woman is believed to have been in her 20s.

Gauteng police spokesperson Colonel Dimakatso Nevhuhulwi said: "The circumstances surrounding the incident are unknown at this stage."

The discovery comes as police investigate a series of cases involving nine women found dead at different locations in Ekurhuleni over two months since July 15. Acting National Commissioner Lt-Gen Puleng Dimpane said all cases are receiving priority attention.

Police appealed to anyone with information to contact nearest police station, call Crime Stop on 08600 10111 or use MySAPS App.

Moroka Dam forms part of Thokoza Park in Rockville, a well-used community space known for lawns, walkways and family picnics - Joburg City Parks.
