Soweto Teen Luyanda (16) Wins Street Lines Skate Comp With 169 Points – From Senaoane Streets to Champ!

Source: EWN / Citizen • Good News Soweto

Big heat, big moves! 16-year-old Luyanda Vukapi from Soweto just showed the world that kasi dreams work. Board under his feet, heart full of passion, he scored 169 points to take Junior Division title at 2026 Street Lines competition.

Locals at Block 88 Concept Store on Lefoka Street – the creative space for youth – say Luyanda used to practice behind Dlamini Multipurpose Centre Hall after school. Now he’s invited to Durban finals.

Funny side: His gogo thought he was “just playing” and asked him to fetch bread mid-competition video call. He landed kickflip while holding plastic bag – now viral on TikTok!

📍 Where to watch: Block 88 Concept Store, 2067 Lefoka St, Soweto – open 11am-5pm daily, live art + skating Saturdays.
